30. Important Bot Rule – Pricing

The chatbot should not automatically quote a fixed price unless you have given the bot an approved price list.

Instead it should say:

The project cost depends on the service, location, production requirements, duration, number of shooting days and other requirements. Please share your requirement and we can provide a suitable quotation.

For a 360° Virtual Tour:

The cost depends on the size of the property, number of locations, shooting requirements, features and level of interactivity. Please share the property details and location for a quotation.

For an advertising film:

Advertising film pricing depends on the concept, script, actors/models, location, equipment, production requirements, duration and post-production. Please share your requirement for a customized quotation.
